LM Knob

Cloud controller firmware for La Marzocco Linea Micra / Mini

If this made your morning coffee ritual a little better, a coffee is always appreciated ☕

☕ Support on Ko-fi
  1. Enter bootloader mode
    Use a needle to hold the small BOOT button on the back of the device, plug it into USB, then release the button.
  2. Flash the firmware — Chrome or Edge only
  3. Wait for flashing to complete, then unplug and replug the USB to reboot the device.
  4. Connect to the setup hotspot
    On first boot, the knob shows a QR code on screen.
    Scan it with your phone — or go to your phone's Wi-Fi settings and connect manually to:
    LM-Knob-Setup  (no password needed)
  5. Open the setup page
    After connecting, a setup page should open automatically on your phone.
    If it doesn't, open your browser and go to 192.168.4.1
  6. Fill in the form — all fields are on one page:
    • Your home Wi-Fi name (SSID) and password
    • La Marzocco account email & password — the same credentials you use in the LM app
    • Machine serial number — found on a sticker on the back of your machine, or in the LM app under Settings → Machine
    • Friendly name (optional) — a custom label shown on the device screen instead of "LA MARZOCCO", e.g. Kitchen Linea Mini. You can change it later in Settings → System → Name.
  7. Submit — you're done!
    The knob reboots, connects to your Wi-Fi, and links to your La Marzocco cloud. The carousel screen appears within a few seconds. ☕

Compatible hardware: Guition JC3636K718C (ESP32-S3, 360×360 round display + knob)
