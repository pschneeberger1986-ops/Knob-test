  1. Plug the knob into USB
  2. Open this page in Chrome or Edge, then click the button below to connect and install

    ⚠️ The button only appears in Chrome or Edge — not Safari or Firefox

  3. Wait for flashing to complete
  4. On first boot, the knob displays a QR code — scan it to connect to LM-Knob-Setup. A setup page will open automatically on your phone
  5. Enter your La Marzocco account credentials and your machine's serial number — a La Marzocco account is required

Compatible hardware: Guition JC3636K718C (ESP32-S3, 360×360 round display + knob)
